About Bangalore

Bangalore, the capital of Karnataka, is one of India's most vibrant and dynamic cities. Known as the "Silicon Valley of India," it has emerged as a global technology hub attracting companies and talent from around the world.

Geography and Climate

Located on the Deccan Plateau at an elevation of about 920 meters, Bangalore enjoys a pleasant climate year-round. The city experiences mild temperatures and occasional rainfall, making it comfortable for residents and visitors alike.
